## Selection

Evidence descriptions distinguish conceptual guidance, descriptive studies, and intervention findings; inclusion does not imply equal evidence strength or proven effectiveness in every setting. Locally developed labels without sufficient external support were not added as established models. Related but distinct frameworks are explained rather than treated as interchangeable.

## Search terms

Search includes model names, acronyms, tags, multiple categories, summaries, section text and references. Explicit keywords include relevant Filipino terms, expanded acronyms and practical applications. Common British/American spellings, punctuation, accents, possessives and selected plurals are normalized. All query words must appear somewhere in the record; names and keyword matches rank first. Clear filters to search all categories. Full-text matching favors discovery and may also surface entries that discuss a related model.

## Resource title and access

Use **SDO Bulacan Research Resources**, with **Research Resources** in the navigation. This identifies the source and purpose more clearly than the generic External Resources. Resource descriptions are included in the portal and Superintendent letter enclosure. The journal has an availability notice, not an invented URL. Public collection access and author submission access are separate arrangements; no sharing settings have been changed.
