Research Management

Understand how research moves through coordination, review, implementation, and use. Use this practical overview together with the current instructions of your school, division, or funding call.

The policy framework

DepEd Order No. 16, s. 2017 establishes research management guidance across governance levels and supports funding, partnerships, capacity building, dissemination, and utilization. DepEd Order No. 26, s. 2021 amends these guidelines, including provisions on remote research activities and financial arrangements.

Confirm the applicable submission route, approving office, required documents, and deadlines with your research coordinator.

A practical research cycle

01

Prepare and coordinate

Clarify the research priority and intended use. Ask the coordinator which proposal format, review process, endorsements, and funding conditions apply.

Keep · The current call, requirements, and your proposed work plan.

02

Submit and respond to review

Submit the proposal and required attachments through the designated channel. Address feedback and retain the revised proposal and written decisions.

Keep · Submission records, reviewer feedback, and the approved version.

03

Implement and monitor

Follow the approved design and applicable permissions. Track progress, protect participant information, and consult the responsible office about changes to the plan.

Keep · Progress records, authorized changes, and financial documents where applicable.

04

Complete and report

Analyze the evidence, explain limitations, and prepare the required research report. Complete applicable reporting and grant-accountability requirements.

Keep · The final report and required completion documents.

05

Share, use, and follow up

Present findings to the people who can use them. Identify a practical decision or improvement, record how the findings inform action, and identify questions for further study.

Keep · A dissemination record and a plan for using the findings.

Documents to confirm before submission

Check which of these apply to your current submission. Requirements may differ by research type and call.

  • Research proposal and research information sheet
  • Applicable action or applied research checklist
  • Required endorsements and supervisor’s conforme
  • Declarations on plagiarism and conflict of interest
  • Instruments, permissions, participant information, and consent or assent materials, as applicable
