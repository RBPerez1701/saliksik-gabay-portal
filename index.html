How to use the portal

  1. Start with evidence. Gather aggregate baseline findings and identify a problem worth studying.
  2. Explore and plan. Read the writing process, review related literature, and consult relevant teaching models when they fit your study.
  3. Develop your proposal. Use the builder to organize your own answers. Individual source cards are optional; you can summarize literature findings directly.
  4. Review and refine. Download the Word draft, complete citations and attachments, and work with your research coordinator on the applicable submission requirements.

Your builder entries are saved in this browser on this device. Download your draft to keep a copy. The builder organizes your input; you supply and verify the evidence, literature, and research decisions.
