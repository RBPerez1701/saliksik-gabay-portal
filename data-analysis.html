Data Analysis

Planning a study? Start with Research Designs. If you already have a design, choose the guide that matches your evidence.

Research Designs

How should I structure my study? Match your question to the participants, timing, comparison and evidence you need.

Quantitative analysis

I have scores, counts or ratings. Describe results, compare groups or examine relationships.

Qualitative analysis

I have interviews, observations or written responses. Choose how to interpret meanings, categories, cases or processes.

Mixed methods

I need to bring numbers and accounts together. Decide when to collect each kind of evidence and how to interpret them together.

One reading program, three possible questions

  • Quantitative. How much did the same learners’ reading scores change from baseline to endline?
  • Qualitative. How do learners describe what helps them attempt an unfamiliar passage?
  • Mixed methods. How do the accounts of learners with different reading gains help explain the pattern in their scores?
